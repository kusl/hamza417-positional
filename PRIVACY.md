# Privacy Policy

Positional is free, open source software distributed through GitHub Releases. It has no accounts, analytics, advertising or crash reporting.

## Data on your device

Location, trails, measurements, saved places and settings are stored only on your device and are removed when you uninstall the app or clear its data.

## Third party services

The map panels use the Google Maps SDK, and the location panels can use Google Play services and the Android Geocoder. These services may receive your location, IP address and device information under Google's privacy policy. All other features work offline.

## Children

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

## Changes

Changes to this policy are published with new releases.
