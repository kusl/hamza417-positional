Required Permissions

Positional uses your location only to show information on your device. It does not collect usage data and does not share or sell any information.

Requested permissions

ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION

Reads precise location from GPS and other providers.

ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION

Shows an approximate location until a precise fix is available.

Automatic permissions

INTERNET

Loads map tiles and converts between coordinates and addresses.

ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE and ACCESS_WIFI_STATE

Checks whether a network is available before loading maps or addresses.
